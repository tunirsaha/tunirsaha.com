Who is Tunir Saha?

Tunir Saha is a full stack developer based in Bengaluru, India, with more than 11 years of experience building web, mobile and cloud applications. He is currently Lead Engineer at FICO, where he architects enterprise micro-frontend platforms with Angular 20, Module Federation and Node.js on AWS. Before FICO he worked at IBM, Accenture, Blue Space Labs, Bridgera and Cognizant, shipping software across finance, logistics, telecom, healthcare, banking, travel and energy. He has been writing production code since 2014.

What is his tech stack?

On the frontend: Angular (versions 2 through 20) and React (15 through 18) with TypeScript, RxJS, NgRx, Redux and Module Federation for micro-frontends. On the backend: Node.js, Express, Python, FastAPI and PHP, serving REST and Socket.IO real-time APIs over PostgreSQL, MySQL and MongoDB. For infrastructure: AWS, Azure and GCP with Docker and CI/CD pipelines. Testing with Jest, Jasmine, Karma, Enzyme and Playwright for end-to-end. He also ships cross-platform mobile apps with Ionic and Capacitor, and has worked on IoT telemetry and embedded firmware.
