Who is Tunir Saha?

Tunir Saha is a full stack developer based in Bengaluru, India, with more than 12 years of experience building web, mobile and cloud applications. He most recently served as Lead Engineer at FICO (Oct 2024 - Oct 2026), where he architected a micro-frontend feature-authoring platform with Angular 20, Module Federation and Node.js on AWS, and won the worldwide AWS Cost Optimization Hackathon. Before FICO he worked at IBM, Accenture, Blue Space Labs, Bridgera and Cognizant, shipping software across finance, logistics, telecom, healthcare, banking, education and hospitality. He has been writing production code since 2014.

What is his tech stack?

On the frontend: Angular (versions 2 through 20) and React (16 through 19) with TypeScript, RxJS, NgRx, Redux, Zustand and Module Federation for micro-frontends. On the backend: Node.js, Express, Python, FastAPI and PHP, serving REST and Socket.IO real-time APIs over PostgreSQL, MySQL and MongoDB. For infrastructure: AWS, Azure and GCP with Docker and GitHub Actions. Testing with Jest, React Testing Library, Jasmine, Karma and Playwright for end-to-end. He also ships cross-platform mobile apps with Ionic, and has worked on IoT telemetry and firmware integration.
